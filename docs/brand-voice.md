# Portfolio brand voice

## Positioning

Muhammad Ubaidullah is an independent full stack developer who connects business goals with the details of the build. The portfolio welcomes founders commissioning a project, businesses improving their digital presence, and teams looking to hire a developer.

Core thought: **Your goals shape what I build.**

## Voice

- Personal: use “I” for Ubaidullah and “we” for collaboration with the visitor.
- Confident: describe the work, decisions, and deliverables in plain language.
- Commercially aware: explain what a capability means for customers or the team using it.
- Approachable: a visitor can start a conversation before they have a finished brief.
- Specific: use documented project examples when explaining experience.

## Section roles

- Services explain what someone can hire Ubaidullah to do.
- Projects show the work and the decisions behind it.
- About introduces the person, working relationship, and approach.
- Technical craft connects the existing technology stack to customer experience, product engineering, and infrastructure.
- Contact welcomes service enquiries and hiring conversations.
- The footer offers a short, human invitation to get in touch.

## Writing rules

Keep headings short and give each section a distinct purpose. Use technical terms where they help someone evaluate the work, with a plain-language explanation for business readers. Prefer direct CTAs such as “Discuss your project,” “See my work,” and “Start a conversation.”

AI automation, generative AI, SEO, and digital marketing are service offers. Describe their uses and scope without implying undocumented client results. Do not add revenue, traffic, ranking, speed, client-count, or experience claims without evidence.

Keep project facts, prices, timelines, and contact details grounded in the existing source or explicit owner updates. Treat hiring enquiries as invitations to discuss a role, without claiming a specific employment arrangement or availability date.
