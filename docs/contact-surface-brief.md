# Contact page

Scope: extend the established portfolio identity for project enquiries and company hiring conversations. The visitor should understand the offer, choose a relevant service or role, and contact Ubaidullah without needing a finished brief. Public contact details and service facts come from the existing project.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A direct conversation with the person doing the work. A generous invitation leads to a focused form and practical next steps.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserve ink, light stone, jade, Space Grotesk and Geist. Semantic theme tokens, thin dividers, 16px form surface corners, legible labels, and slower rolling text links carry the existing identity.

**STORY:** Understand the kinds of work, share enough context to begin, and know how the conversation proceeds. Direct email and social contacts stay available.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Large two-line invitation on the left; a geometric jade connection flowing around the personal “u.” on the right; an availability/location rail below. The “Tell me about it” link goes directly to the form. Mobile prioritizes the form before the direct contact details.

**FORM:** Code-led extension of the existing portfolio system; no new-world seed or approved comparison image. Signature interaction: a continuous jade trace, intro-aware heading reveal, slower text flips, and a scroll-linked next-step line. Form fields remain still while typing. Respect reduced motion.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Quality bar

- Sans-serif only; consistent desktop width and jade identity in both themes.
- Form labels remain visible, inputs are 16px, touch controls are at least 44px, and keyboard focus is clear.
- Hiring selection changes the message prompt and omits project budget.
- Loading prevents duplicate submissions; errors retain the visitor’s values and provide direct email recovery.
- Only an accepted owner notification permits success. An acknowledgment failure must not invite another send.
- No made-up client outcomes or availability promises beyond the existing portfolio facts.
- No build command. Check targeted lint, TypeScript, mail transport tests, and desktop/mobile browser evidence.
