# Brag Plan: ubaid.dev

## What is this app?
A personal portfolio for websites, custom software, and AI workflows built around the way a business works.

## The angle
Build. Automate. Grow. The impressive part is the connection between a business's first impression and the systems behind it. Show the real commerce interface as proof of engineering and the portfolio's signature jade sculpture as its visual identity.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
“What people see. How your business runs.” Two large, readable lines, revealed in order over the portfolio's signature artwork.

## Key moments
- Real E-Commerce Platform + CMS product inventory screen, with products, stock, and orders called out.
- A clearly labeled example AI workflow: Enquiry → Context → AI draft → Human review. This illustrates services, not a delivered client result.
- SEO and digital marketing as the discovery and growth side of the service offering.

## Outro
The signature sculpture, ubaid.dev, and “Good work starts with a conversation.” End on a readable invitation to start a conversation.

## User flow worth showing
None — this is a portfolio website, not a transactional app. The browsing story is work → capabilities → contact. The real delivered commerce interface supplies product evidence.

## Tone
- Preset: polished, with cinematic material depth.
- Direction: confident editorial typography, precision motion, titanium and jade.
- Interpretation: readable holds, deliberate beat changes, sparse audio accents, no novelty transitions or inflated performance claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 22 seconds, 30 fps

## Visual identity
- Background: #0A0E1A
- Accent: #00D9A6
- Text: #F8F7F4
- Secondary text: #9CA3AF
- Display font: Space Grotesk
- Body font: Geist
- Strongest visual: public/images/services/connected-signature.webp
- Proof asset: public/images/projects/ecommerce-platform/cover.png
- Sans-serif only, as requested. No names, testimonials, credentials, or made-up results.

## Share copy
A new chapter for my portfolio: websites, custom software, and AI workflows, built around the way a business works. Build. Automate. Grow.

## Audio direction
- Role: clean music bed with sparse professional accents; no voiceover.
- Music: Happy Beats — Business Moves Vol. 12, bundled with Brag.
- Treatment: source begins at 0; bed at 0.34 gain; 0.4-second opening fade and 1.4-second closing fade.
- Cue guidance: bundled cue notes read. Major beats 8.74, 13.11, 17.47 seconds; opener hands off at 3.27. Workflow steps arrive on 9.29, 9.83, 10.37, 10.93.
- Reactive treatment: pre-extracted bass lightly changes a jade architectural edge glow. No music visualization.
- SFX: two soft material impacts and two quiet interface accents.
- Restraint: no constant clicks, distracting high-frequency sounds, or music-driven text movement.
